OxfordAQA International Science – Grade 8

Unit Aims
- Develop understanding of cells, respiration, and ecosystems.
- Apply evidence-based reasoning to scientific questions.

Learning Outcomes
- Describe cell structures and compare specialized cells.
- Explain aerobic respiration and gas exchange.
- Interpret food webs and human impacts on ecosystems.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners explain how organelles support function.
- Learners analyze ecosystem changes using data.

Key Vocabulary
- organelle, respiration, diffusion, ecosystem, interdependence

Common Misconceptions
- Believing plants only photosynthesize and do not respire.
- Confusing food chains with food webs.
