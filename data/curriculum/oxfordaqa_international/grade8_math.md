OxfordAQA International Mathematics – Grade 8

Unit Aims
- Strengthen algebraic reasoning and geometry problem solving.

Learning Outcomes
- Simplify expressions and solve linear equations.
- Use ratio and proportion in multi-step problems.
- Apply angle properties in triangles and parallel lines.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners show working clearly and explain steps.

Key Vocabulary
- expression, equation, ratio, proportion, angle

Common Misconceptions
- Cancelling terms incorrectly in equations.
- Using additive strategies for multiplicative problems.
