OxfordAQA International English – Grade 7

Unit Aims
- Develop reading comprehension and structured writing.

Learning Outcomes
- Identify main ideas and supporting evidence.
- Explain author purpose and viewpoint.
- Write cohesive paragraphs with clear structure.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners paraphrase accurately and use text evidence.

Key Vocabulary
- inference, evidence, purpose, cohesion

Common Misconceptions
- Copying text without interpretation.
