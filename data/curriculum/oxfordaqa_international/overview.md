OxfordAQA International – Lower Secondary Overview

Aims
- Build rigorous subject knowledge with international benchmarking.
- Emphasize analytical thinking and communication.

Progression Expectations
- Learners move from structured tasks to independent reasoning.
- Accurate vocabulary and clear explanation are required.

Assessment Focus
- Regular checks for understanding, unit tests, and extended responses.

Key Vocabulary Expectations
- Precise terminology in oral and written responses.

Common Misconceptions (General)
- Treating examples as rules.
- Overlooking units in quantitative problems.
