Assessment Skills – Lower Secondary

Aims
- Build reliable routines for checks for understanding and formative assessment.

Progression
- Grade 7: short recall checks and guided explanations.
- Grade 8: structured reasoning with evidence and justification.

Assessment Statements
- Learners respond to hinge questions and explain choices.
- Learners reflect on misconceptions and correct them.

Key Vocabulary
- criteria, evidence, justification, misconception, feedback

Common Misconceptions
- Treating feedback as final grades rather than guidance.
