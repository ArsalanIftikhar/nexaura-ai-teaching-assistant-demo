Grade 7 English – Reading, Writing, Speaking

Unit Aims
- Build comprehension of narrative and informational texts.
- Develop structured paragraph writing and oral communication.

Learning Outcomes
- Identify main ideas, supporting details, and author’s purpose.
- Use evidence from text to justify answers.
- Write a paragraph with topic sentence, evidence, and conclusion.
- Use accurate punctuation and varied sentence structures.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners annotate a passage and summarize key points.
- Learners draft a paragraph using a planning frame.

Key Vocabulary
- inference, evidence, summary, conclusion, viewpoint

Common Misconceptions
- Copying large sections instead of paraphrasing.
- Mixing tenses in a single paragraph.
