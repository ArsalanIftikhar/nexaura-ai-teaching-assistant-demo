Grade 8 Mathematics – Algebra, Ratio, Geometry

Unit Aims
- Strengthen algebraic manipulation and proportional reasoning.
- Apply geometry properties to solve multi-step problems.

Learning Outcomes
- Simplify algebraic expressions with like terms.
- Solve one- and two-step linear equations.
- Use ratio and proportion in word problems.
- Calculate angles in parallel lines and triangles.
- Use area and perimeter for composite shapes.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners solve equations and justify each step.
- Learners explain ratio reasoning in contextual problems.

Key Vocabulary
- expression, equation, coefficient, ratio, proportion
- complementary, supplementary, parallel, transversal

Common Misconceptions
- Treating terms with different powers as like terms.
- Dividing only one term in an expression.
- Misinterpreting ratio as subtraction.
