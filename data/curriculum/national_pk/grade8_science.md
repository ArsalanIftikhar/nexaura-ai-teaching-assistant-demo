Grade 8 Science – Cells, Respiration, Ecosystems

Unit Aims
- Explain cell structure and how organelles support function.
- Describe aerobic respiration and gas exchange.
- Analyze interdependence in ecosystems and human impacts.

Learning Outcomes
- Identify nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria, chloroplasts, and vacuole.
- Compare specialized cells (red blood cell, root hair cell, palisade cell).
- State the word equation for aerobic respiration and apply it to simple scenarios.
- Explain diffusion in alveoli and the role of surface area and blood supply.
- Construct food chains and interpret effects of population changes.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners label a cell diagram accurately and justify each organelle’s role.
- Learners explain how structure supports function in two specialized cells.
- Learners write a short paragraph explaining respiration and gas exchange.

Key Vocabulary
- cell membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria, chloroplast, vacuole
- respiration, oxygen, glucose, energy, diffusion, alveoli
- ecosystem, producer, consumer, predator, prey, population

Common Misconceptions
- Thinking plants do not respire.
- Confusing breathing with cellular respiration.
- Assuming all food chains are linear with no overlap.
