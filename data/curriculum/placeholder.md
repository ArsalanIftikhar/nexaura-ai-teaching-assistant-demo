# Placeholder Curriculum v0.1 (Key Stage 3 Science)

This placeholder excerpt is for demo purposes only. It represents Year 8 Science topics focused on cells, respiration, and ecosystems.

## Unit: Cells and Organisation

Students should be able to describe the structure and function of animal and plant cells, including the role of the nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria, and chloroplasts.

Students should compare specialised cells (e.g., red blood cells, root hair cells, palisade cells) and explain how structure supports function.

## Unit: Respiration and Gas Exchange

Students should explain aerobic respiration as a chemical reaction requiring oxygen and glucose to release energy. Students should know the word equation and be able to apply it to simple scenarios.

Students should describe gas exchange in the lungs, including the role of the alveoli and the diffusion of oxygen and carbon dioxide.

## Unit: Ecosystems and Interdependence

Students should define ecosystems and explain interdependence between organisms, including food chains, food webs, and the impact of changes in population size.

Students should explain how human activity can disrupt ecosystems and propose simple conservation strategies.
