Cambridge Lower Secondary English – Grade 7

Unit Aims
- Develop comprehension and structured writing for different purposes.

Learning Outcomes
- Identify main ideas and supporting details.
- Use evidence to explain interpretations.
- Write coherent paragraphs with clear structure.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners summarize a text with accurate detail.
- Learners draft persuasive paragraphs using evidence.

Key Vocabulary
- inference, evidence, paragraph, cohesion

Common Misconceptions
- Confusing opinion with evidence.
