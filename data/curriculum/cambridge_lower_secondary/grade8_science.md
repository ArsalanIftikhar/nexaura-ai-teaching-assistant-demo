Cambridge Lower Secondary Science – Grade 8

Unit Aims
- Understand cells and body systems, energy changes, and ecosystems.
- Apply scientific thinking to explain observations.

Learning Outcomes
- Describe cell structures and compare plant and animal cells.
- Explain respiration as an energy-releasing process.
- Describe interactions in food webs and effects of change.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners produce labeled diagrams and explain function.
- Learners predict outcomes when populations change.

Key Vocabulary
- organelle, respiration, diffusion, ecosystem, population

Common Misconceptions
- Confusing photosynthesis with respiration.
- Assuming bigger animals are always predators.
