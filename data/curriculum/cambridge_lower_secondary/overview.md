Cambridge Lower Secondary – Overview

Aims
- Develop confident learners with curiosity, communication, and transferable skills.
- Emphasize inquiry, reasoning, and application across subjects.

Progression Expectations
- Learners progress from simple recall to analysis and explanation.
- Structured language and precise vocabulary are expected in responses.

Assessment Focus
- Formative tasks, low-stakes quizzes, and performance tasks aligned to learning objectives.

Key Vocabulary Expectations
- Accurate use of subject terms in explanations and written responses.

Common Misconceptions (General)
- Overgeneralizing from a single example.
- Using informal language in academic writing.
