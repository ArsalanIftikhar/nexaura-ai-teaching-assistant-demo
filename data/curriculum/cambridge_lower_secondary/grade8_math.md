Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics – Grade 8

Unit Aims
- Extend algebraic reasoning and proportional thinking.

Learning Outcomes
- Manipulate expressions and solve linear equations.
- Apply ratio to compare quantities and solve problems.
- Use angle properties in polygons and parallel lines.

Indicative Assessment Statements
- Learners justify algebraic steps in writing.
- Learners solve ratio problems with units.

Key Vocabulary
- simplify, equation, ratio, angle, polygon

Common Misconceptions
- Treating ratios as additive rather than multiplicative.
